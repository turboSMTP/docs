Manage account-wide email options in the [turboSMTP dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com).

## Unsubscribe links, footers, and templates

Open **Settings → Email elements** to find **Unsubscribe link**, **Footers**, and **Templates**. Use these controls to configure the email elements available for your account.

Configure an unsubscribe option for your marketing messages and review the resulting message before sending a campaign. Review opt-outs under **Suppressions**. See [bounce and unsubscribe management](/deliverability/bounce-spam-management).

## Global BCC recipients

Open **Settings → Notifications → BCCs** to configure the account's global BCC list. These recipients receive copies of outgoing mail. Review this list when troubleshooting unexpected copies of messages.

The global list is separate from the `bcc` recipients you provide for an individual [API send](/sending/email-api).

## Tracking and Google Analytics

Open **Settings → Tracking** for open and click tracking, the **Google Analytics** toggle, and **Custom Tracking Domains**.

See [email tracking](/analytics/email-tracking) for tracking controls and measurement limits, and [custom tracking domains](/deliverability/custom-tracking-domain) for DNS setup and plan allowances.

## Email marketing add-on

Open **Addons** to access **Email marketing** and **Email validator**. Check availability and any purchase requirements shown for your account. See [email validation](/account/email-validation) for list-cleaning workflows.

## Notifications

Open **Settings → Notifications** to configure:

- **Activity reports** for daily, weekly, or monthly summaries.
- **Bounces Management** to choose who receives bounce notifications.
- **Webhooks → Edit** for event callbacks.

See [statistics](/analytics/statistics-dashboard), [bounce management](/deliverability/bounce-spam-management), and [webhooks](/analytics/webhooks) for details.
