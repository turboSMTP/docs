turboSMTP offers flexible pricing designed to fit any sending volume — from a free plan that covers most small projects to high-volume monthly subscriptions and pay-as-you-go blocks for variable senders. Every plan gives you access to the same deliverability infrastructure, tracking features, and 24/7 support; you simply choose the volume and billing model that suits your workflow.

## Plan types

turboSMTP offers two distinct billing models:

**Monthly subscription plans** renew automatically each month. Choose a monthly plan if you send email consistently and want predictable billing. Monthly plans include the sub-account feature on Pro tiers and above, and Professional/Enterprise tiers include 3,000 email validations per month.

**Prepaid / pay-as-you-go plans** let you purchase a fixed block of emails that never expire. Choose a prepaid plan if your sending volume is irregular or seasonal. You pay once and draw down the credit balance at your own pace, with no recurring charge.

## Free plan

The turboSMTP free plan gives you everything you need to get started — no credit card required:

- **6,000 emails per month** (resets on your monthly anniversary)
- **200 emails per day** sending limit
- Access to all core features: tracking, analytics, bounce management, API access
- No time limit — the free plan does not expire

Sign up at [serversmtp.com/en/signup](https://serversmtp.com/en/signup) and start sending within minutes.

## Upgrade or change plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your dashboard">
    Go to [dashboard.serversmtp.com](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the plan selector">
    Click your account name or avatar in the top-right corner of the dashboard and select **Change Plan** (or **Upgrade**) from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your new plan">
    Browse the available monthly and prepaid plans. Select the plan that matches your sending volume and billing preference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Enter your payment details and confirm the purchase. Your new plan activates immediately and your email credit balance updates right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Note>

## Auto-renewal

Monthly subscription plans **renew automatically** on the same date each month. The charge is processed by 2Checkout using the payment method you provided at signup.

**To cancel auto-renewal:**

1. Go to the [2Checkout account page](https://secure.avangate.com/myaccount/) and log in using your order number and email address.
2. Navigate to **My Services**.
3. Click **Stop automatic license renewal** beneath the turboSMTP plan.

Alternatively, contact turboSMTP support (available 24/7 via ticket, chat, or phone) and the team will process the cancellation for you. Cancelling auto-renewal does not immediately deactivate your plan — you retain access through the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

## Money-back guarantee

<Note>
  turboSMTP offers a **full refund within 15 days of purchase** or after **100 emails have been sent**, whichever comes first. To request a refund, contact turboSMTP support within the guarantee window. Refund requests are processed promptly and no reason is required.
</Note>

## Invoices

When you complete a purchase, your payment gateway (2Checkout) issues an invoice automatically. The invoice is sent to the email address on your account and is also available in your 2Checkout account portal.

If the dashboard shows your invoice as **unpaid** immediately after completing payment, wait a few minutes. The gateway takes a short time to process and confirm the transaction before the status updates. You will receive a confirmation email from the payment gateway once the payment is fully processed.

If you have not received a confirmation email within one hour of payment, contact turboSMTP support and the team will investigate the transaction on your behalf.
