## Get started

Follow these steps to create your account and start sending:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [serversmtp.com/en/signup](https://serversmtp.com/en/signup) and fill in the registration form with your name, email address, and a password. Click **Sign Up** to submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from turboSMTP. Click the confirmation link inside to verify your address. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for account activation">
    Every new free account is reviewed by turboSMTP staff before activation. This is a quick quality check to ensure the platform remains healthy for all senders. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to your dashboard">
    Once activated, log in at [dashboard.serversmtp.com](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com). From here you can configure SMTP settings, generate API keys, monitor delivery statistics, and manage your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Account activation is reviewed manually by turboSMTP staff. You'll receive an email notification as soon as your account is activated. Most activations are completed promptly, but allow some time during busy periods.
</Note>

## What's included in the free tier

Your free plan gives you immediate access to everything you need to start sending:

- **6,000 emails per month** (with a maximum send rate of 200 emails per day)
- **Full dashboard access** at [dashboard.serversmtp.com](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) — manage settings, view logs, and monitor activity
- **SMTP relay** — connect any email client or application using standard SMTP credentials
- **REST API access** — send and manage emails programmatically via the turboSMTP API
- **Real-time delivery tracking** — monitor opens, clicks, bounces, spam complaints, and unsubscribes
- **Email validation** — screen your contact list for invalid or risky addresses before sending
- **24/7 support** — access to ticket, chat, and phone support

## Free vs paid plans

Both free and paid plans use the same delivery infrastructure. The main differences are sending volume, daily rate limits, and support priority.

| Feature | Free Plan | Paid Plans |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly email allowance | 6,000 emails | From 10,000 up to millions |
| Daily send limit | 200 emails/day | Higher limits based on plan |
| Account activation | Staff review required | Faster activation after payment |
| API access | ✓ | ✓ |
| Real-time tracking | ✓ | ✓ |
| Email validation | ✓ | ✓ |
| Support | Standard (24/7) | Priority support on higher tiers |

<Note>
  When you have both a free allowance and a paid plan active, turboSMTP uses your free emails first (up to 200/day), then draws from your paid quota.
</Note>

## Upgrade your plan

When you're ready to send more than 6,000 emails per month or need higher daily throughput, upgrading is straightforward. Browse all available monthly and prepaid plans at [serversmtp.com/en/prices](https://serversmtp.com/en/prices) and select the volume that fits your needs.

To upgrade from within your dashboard:

1. Log in to [dashboard.serversmtp.com](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com).
2. Click your account name in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Upgrade Plan** and choose from the available options.
4. Complete the purchase — your new quota activates after payment is confirmed.
