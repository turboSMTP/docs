The Free plan lets you evaluate SMTP relay and HTTP API sending with up to **200 emails per day**. No credit card is required.

The current [pricing page](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/) lists the offer for the **first six months**. Review the offer shown during signup and the plan details in your dashboard.

## Activate your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Follow **Start for free** on the [pricing page](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/), select **Register** on the sign-in screen, and complete registration. The sign-in screen also offers Google and Microsoft sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the email instructions">
    Check your inbox and complete the verification steps sent by turboSMTP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for activation">
    turboSMTP staff review eligible free accounts. You receive an email when activation is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create sending credentials">
    Sign in to the [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) and open **Settings → Integrations → API Keys → Edit**. Create a key with **SMTP Sending** or **HTTP API Sending** enabled for your integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the **Consumer Key** as your SMTP username and the **Consumer Secret** as your SMTP password. For HTTP sending, pass them as the `consumerKey` and `consumerSecret` headers. See [API keys](/get-started/api-keys) and [SMTP configuration](/get-started/smtp-configuration).

## Free plan features and limits

| Feature | Free allowance |
|---|---|
| Sending | Up to 200 emails per day |
| SMTP relay and HTTP API | Included |
| API keys | 2 |
| Email validation | 100 credits, separate from sending credits |
| Analytics | 30-day demo |
| Custom tracking domains | 30-day demo |
| Webhooks | 30-day demo; available events depend on your account |
| Support | Chat and ticket |

Demo features have their own availability window. See [plans and billing](/account/plans-billing) for paid-plan allowances, including analytics retention and phone support.

## Upgrade

Basic starts at **15,000 emails per month**. Compare Basic, Pro, Enterprise, and prepaid options on the [pricing page](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/).

In the dashboard, click the green **Order a new plan** button in the top bar or use the order option in your plan box. Your purchase activates after payment approval and any required account review.

If your account has both free and paid sending allowances, turboSMTP uses the available free allowance first, up to 200 emails per day, before using paid volume.
