## How To Be In Compliance With GDPR

**You are the controller of your personal data** and turboSMTP processes personal data on your behalf.

To help in your activities, we offer you the safest and most reliable tools, which are compliant to GDPR.

We invite you to take a look at the following advices so that you learn what to do to comply with the new privacy provisions and how to correctly use turboSMTP:

- **Protect Your PC with a Password and antivirus**: nobody else can have access to your data
- You will have to **pay attention to our new conditions of data processing**, updated to comply with the GDPR
- We suggest you to **change regularly your login password**
- When you export your data from turboSMTP, **make sure your data is protected**!
- **If you use a third party API to import your data**, make sure you have an update contract about data processing comply with the GDPR
