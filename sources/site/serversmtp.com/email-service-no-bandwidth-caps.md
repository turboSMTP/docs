# Source: https://serversmtp.com/email-service-no-bandwidth-caps

Our email delivery system has no bandwidth caps: all your newsletters will be sent using **the whole band at our disposal**, without limits put on data transfer.

That means that the speed of email sending depends uniquely on external factors such as:

- the **weight** of your newsletter (it’s better to keep it under 70 Kb);
- the speed of your own **internet connection;**
- the capacity of your **email client** to process data (we suggest to use a professional one like [SendBlaster](http://www.sendblaster.com/));
- the performance of your **computer**.
