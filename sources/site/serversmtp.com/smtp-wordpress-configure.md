## Frequently Asked Questions about WordPress SMTP and email issues.

1. ##### Why is WordPress not sending emails?

 WordPress uses PHP mail by default, which isn’t reliable for sending emails. Many hosting providers block or limit PHP mail due to spam concerns, leading to email delivery issues for notifications, contact forms, or WooCommerce emails. Configuring SMTP helps improve deliverability by authenticating emails through a dedicated mail server.

2. ##### What is SMTP, and why do I need it for WordPress?

 SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) is a protocol for sending emails over the internet. Using SMTP with WordPress ensures that your emails are authenticated and sent through a trusted server, increasing the chances of reaching your recipients’ inboxes rather than being marked as spam.

3. ##### How do I set up SMTP for WordPress?

 To set up SMTP: 
 – Install an SMTP plugin, such as TurboSMTP, in the WordPress dashboard. 
 – Configure the plugin by entering your SMTP server details (e.g., Gmail, Office 365, or your email provider). 
 – Test the configuration to make sure emails are sent correctly.

5. ##### Why are WordPress emails being marked as spam?

 Emails may be flagged as spam due to factors like using a shared IP with a bad reputation, not having proper authentication (SPF or DKIM records), or including content that triggers spam filters. Configuring SMTP can help by providing authentication and sending emails through a trusted server.

6. ##### How can I test if my WordPress SMTP settings are working?

 After configuring the SMTP plugin, send a test email using the plugin’s built-in testing feature. This will confirm that the emails are sent successfully and are delivered to the intended recipient’s inbox.
