### 12\. I have more than one email address. Should I open multiple accounts?

No, you just need **to create one single account**. With turboSMTP you will be able to send emails from any of your email addresses, using the same account.

Just make sure to [configure your mail client properly](https://www.serversmtp.com/en/smtp/Client-Configuration/kb-1 'client configuration turboSMTP').
