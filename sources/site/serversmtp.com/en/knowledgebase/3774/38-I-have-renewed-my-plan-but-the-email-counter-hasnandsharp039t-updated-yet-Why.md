### 38\. I have renewed my plan, but the email counter hasn't updated yet. Why?

Your turboSMTP dashboard features a counter to help you easily track **emails sent** and **emails remaining** within your plan. 

This counter updates **after you send an email**. So, to ensure you're seeing the most current figures, particularly after a **plan renewal** (which includes a **reset of your counter** for the new period), simply send one email. 

This counter provides a clear overview of your current subscription's usage.
