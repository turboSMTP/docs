### 15\. What if I want to cancel my monthly plan?

In order to ensure an **uninterrupted service**, all monthly plans will be **automatically renewed** at the end of the plan's Billing Cycle (monthly or annual). 

You can **remove your monthly plan at any time** by cancelling your subscription directly within [your turboSMTP account](https://www.serversmtp.com/en/clientarea.php).

To avoid automatic billing for a subsequent billing cycle, you must **submit your online cancellation request** at least **15 days** prior to your billing date.

Cancellation requests submitted fewer than 15 days prior to a billing date may result in automatic billing for one (1) additional billing cycle.
