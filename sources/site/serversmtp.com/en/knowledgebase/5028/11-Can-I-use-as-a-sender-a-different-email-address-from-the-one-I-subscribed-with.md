### 11\. Can I use as a sender a different email address from the one I subscribed with?

Yes, you can: you can put as a sender address whichever email address you want and keep using turboSMTP.

Remember anyhow that you must sign up with a [**proprietary email address**](https://serversmtp.com/en/smtp/Have-your-own-domain-as-a-sender/kb-3848.html) (i.e., info@yoursite.com) and configure your DKIM/SPF records accordingly.
