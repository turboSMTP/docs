### 31\. How can I get notified of my bounce?

Want to keep a close eye on your bounce notifications? You can easily choose how to receive them directly in your inbox or let turboSMTP manage them for you.

Here’s how to set your preferences.

- Log in to your [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com). 

- Click on the **Settings** tab on the left-hand menu. 

 ![Bounces menu](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/bounces-menu-en.png) 

- Select **Notifications** from the options. and disable "**Bounces management**" if you prefer to receive all bounce notifications directly. 

 ![Bounces menu](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/bounces-settings-en.png)
