### Knowledgebase

### 1\. What is turboSMTP?

TurboSMTP is a **professional and secure SMTP service** designed to deliver any type of email with **high deliverability**. 
It offers reliable servers and white-listed IPs to improve the delivery of your emails.

The service includes **24/7 customer support**, real-time **reporting**, and **flexible plans** based on the volume of emails sent. 
TurboSMTP also handles the secure delivery of **transactional emails** and provides **APIs for developers**.
