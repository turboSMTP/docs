### 14\. How can I cancel the auto-renewal of my plan?

If you have paid via **2Checkout**, [go to your account page](https://secure.avangate.com/myaccount/ 'avangate') and login with your order number and email, then go to the "My Services" area. 
Once there, simply click "**Stop automatic license renewal**" beneath each product that has this option enabled.
