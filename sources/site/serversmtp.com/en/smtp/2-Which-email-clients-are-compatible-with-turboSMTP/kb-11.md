### 2\. Which email clients are compatible with turboSMTP?

All major email clients are **compatible with turboSMTP**, including all versions of Microsoft Outlook, Microsoft Vista Mail, Microsoft Outlook Express, Mozilla Thunderbird, Opera Mail, Netscape Mail, Apple iPhone, and many more.
