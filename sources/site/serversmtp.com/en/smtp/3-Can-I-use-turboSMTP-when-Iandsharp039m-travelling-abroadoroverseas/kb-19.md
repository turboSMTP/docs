### 3\. Can I use turboSMTP when I'm travelling abroad/overseas?

Of course, you can **keep sending emails** with turboSMTP just as you do at home. 

[Set it up once](https://www.serversmtp.com/en/smtp/Email-client-Set-up-General-Information/kb-36.html 'turboSMTP setup'), and it will work perfectly **with any broadband, mobile and corporate network in the world**.
