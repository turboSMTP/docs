### 35\. My ISP blocks outgoing mail on port 25. What can I do?

In case port 25 (the default port for SMTP) is blocked you can choose an **alternate port**. We suggest to use **port 587 or 2525 for plain SMTP**, and **port 465 or 25025** when using SMTP with SSL.
