### 9\. How can I change my password?

Changing your password is a quick and easy way to **keep your account secure**. Here's how to do it:

- Log in to your [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com). 

- Click on **your name** in the top-right corner of the screen and select "**My Account**" from the dropdown menu. 

 ![change password](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/change-pwd-menu-en.png) 

- Click on "**Change Password**" to update your password. 

 ![change password](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/change-pwd-en.png)
