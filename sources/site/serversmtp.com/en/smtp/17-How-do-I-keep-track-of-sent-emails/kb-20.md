### 17\. How do I keep track of sent emails?

With turboSMTP you can keep track of **everything happens to your sent messages** (delivery, bounce, opening, spam complaint etc.). 

After logging in to your [advanced client area](http://dashboard.serversmtp.com/ 'advanced stats area'), select the "Statistics" menu. You will get both a chart visualizing the behaviour of all your sent emails, and a list with all details recipient by recipient. Both are **updated in real-time**.

The list includes the following information:

- Subject
- Sender ("From" email address)
- Recipient "To", "cc", "Bcc" email address)
- Time (Date & Time received by our servers)
- Status (Queued - Delivered - Opened - Clicked - Unsubscribed - Spam - Bounce - Dropped).
