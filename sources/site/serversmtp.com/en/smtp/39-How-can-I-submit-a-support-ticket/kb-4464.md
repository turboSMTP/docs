### 39\. How can I submit a support ticket?

To get assistance, here's how you can quickly open a support ticket from your dashboard.

- Log in to your [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com). 

- Click the **question mark (?)** icon at the top of the pageand select "**My Tickets**" from the dropdown menu. 

 ![show tickets](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/tickets-en.png) 

- Click on "**Submit a Ticket**" to create a new support request. 

 ![show tickets](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/tickets-new-en.png)
