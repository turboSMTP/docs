### 13\. I want to change my plan. Can I do it?

You can order a new plan by following the steps below: 

1. Log in to your **[dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com)**.

2. Click on **your name** in the top right corner of the screen and select "**My Products**" from the dropdown menu. 

![change plan 1](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/change-plan-en.png)

3. Click "**Order New Plan**" to proceed. 

![change plan 2](https://serversmtp.com/clients/images/kb/change-plan-buy-en.png)
