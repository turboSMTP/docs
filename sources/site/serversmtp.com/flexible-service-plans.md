# Source: https://serversmtp.com/flexible-service-plans

Not all email campaigns are created equal! Either you need to send a **single amount of emails** with a one-off payment, or you prefer to set up a **monthly subscription**, turboSMTP offers you a wide range of [different packages](https://serversmtp.com/en/cart.php?systpl=turbo-smtp&currency=3 'turboSMTP pricing') created to meet your needs.

For instance, you can buy 10.000 emails per month – and then renew your subscription as long as you prefer – or take a pay-as-you-go set, if you don’t need the same amount for each period (or you want to dilute your relays). You can also combine the two.

Moreover, you can change your plan or **upgrade your account** in every moment with a simple click, directly in your client area – buying more relays that will be simply added to your current plan.

Finally, our advanced dashboard allows you to **set an alert** that will be delivered to your email address **when you reach a certain percentage of the whole credit**. So you won’t ever risk to run out of relays in the middle of a mailout.
