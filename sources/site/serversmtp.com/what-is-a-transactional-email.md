## What transactional emails are and why they are so important for your business.

Transactional emails are those messages sent after an action taken by the customer. To give you some examples, we’re talking about emails sent after subscribing to a list, following the purchase of a product or service, when a password reset is requested, or when someone wants to delete their account.

These are mostly communications for order and payment confirmations, password or data changes. 

So, we’re talking about **emails that are extremely important for both the customer and your business**. In fact, they are crucial for your brand’s credibility: their timeliness and accuracy contribute to building the reputation of your brand and business.

Imagine placing an order on an e-commerce website and not receiving an email confirmation of the purchase. The company in question may be legitimate, but you might start to have doubts about its reliability.

Transactional emails go beyond conveying practical information; they also signal that the company is trustworthy, a quality that is increasingly important for web users.

Transactional emails that don’t arrive (or arrive too late) can be detrimental to your business, as it would lose credibility in the eyes of potential new customers.

They serve as a kind of business card for your brand, which is why they should always reach their intended recipients.

## Commercial emails vs. transactional emails: what are the main differences?

There are significant differences between commercial emails (or newsletters) and transactional emails.

On one hand, email marketing represents an additional tool to develop your business, while on the other hand, **transactional emails are indispensable for your business**, and doing without them would inevitably harm the credibility of your brand.

Transactional email is the most important one-to-one communication tool between a brand and a customer. Commercial emails, on the other hand, are generally massively sent to a contact list at scheduled times and days. In other words, commercial emails are not part of one-to-one communication but rather one-to-many.

Certainly, in the modern era, you can’t think of giving up email marketing. However, implementing effective, timely, and precise transactional communication is undoubtedly the fundamental starting point for a comprehensive strategy.

## 5 types of transactional emails you should know.

Transactional emails can be categorized into 5 types:

1. Confirmation emails for successful subscriptions.
2. Order confirmation emails.
3. Notification emails (such as those for order tracking or new message notifications).
4. Password recovery emails.
5. Emails containing invoices and receipts.

Let’s take a closer look at them below 👇.

### 1\. Confirmation emails for successful subscriptions.

[![Transactional emails subscription confirm][base64-image]](https://serversmtp.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/transactional-emails-subscription_confirm.jpg)

Creating an account is typically the first interaction between a brand and a customer. By providing their information to create an account on your website, people are expressing an interest in what you offer.

But what should you do at this point?

You’ll need to ensure that the transactional email containing the confirmation of successful registration reaches the recipient immediately.

If the registration mechanism you’ve chosen is double opt-in, meaning a double confirmation, the recipient should first receive the email to confirm their email address and then the confirmation of their subscription.

### 2\. Order confirmation emails.

[![Transactional order confirmation emails][base64-image]](https://serversmtp.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/transactional-emails-order_confirmed.jpg)

Do you remember? It’s important for users to have trust in your brand if you want to lead them to purchase and loyalty. And this is a fundamental aspect, regardless of the products or services you offer.

Certainly, there are many ways to ensure a good reputation, but among the most important is undoubtedly sending transactional order confirmation emails.

This type of communication must reach its destination immediately, as soon as the customer completes their request. Your punctuality will convey professionalism and reliability.

### 3\. Notification emails.

[![Transactional notification emails][base64-image]](https://serversmtp.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/transactional-emails-order_status.jpg)

Among transactional emails, we also find all the so-called notification emails, which are those email messages containing shipment alerts, potential delays, and package tracking links.

Email notifications of messages are also included in this category, which arrive when a message has been received from a seller.

Therefore, these are all the emails that contain an update to communicate to the user.

### 4\. Password recovery emails.

[![Transactional emails reset password][base64-image]](https://serversmtp.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/transactional-emails-reset_password.jpg)

It may have happened to you as well to register on a website and forget your login credentials. Similarly, it could also happen to your customers and subscribers.

In the event that a customer needs to request the password reset and change procedure, they should immediately receive a transactional email that allows them to complete the process by clicking on the received link.

### 5\. Emails containing invoices and receipts.

[![Transactional emails invoice][base64-image]](https://serversmtp.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/transactional-emails-invoice.jpg)

Certainly, it’s often possible to send payment receipts and invoices along with the order confirmation email. However, there are cases where it’s better to send these documents in a separate transactional email.

An example?

Car-sharing services, which send the invoice via email at the end of the ride, and subscription services, which send a dedicated invoice at regular intervals.

1\. **Sending limitations**: personal email accounts often impose limits on the number of emails you can send within a specific time frame. These limits may not be sufficient if you need to send transactional emails to many people simultaneously. On the other hand, a professional transactional email service allows you to avoid these limitations.

2\. **Authentication issues**: sending transactional emails often requires the use of specific authentication protocols, such as SPF (Sender Policy Framework) and DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail), to ensure that emails are not marked as spam.
