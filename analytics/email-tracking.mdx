When enabled, open and click tracking records interactions with your messages. Review these events alongside delivery status in **Analytics**.

## How tracking works

**Open tracking** adds a small image to the HTML body. Loading that image records an open event. It requires HTML content and an email client that loads images.

**Click tracking** rewrites supported links through a tracking URL, records the request, and redirects to the original destination.

An open is evidence that the image loaded, rather than proof that a person read the message. Image blocking, [privacy features](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/protect-email-privacy-mlhlp1205/mac), and [automated link checks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-office-365/safe-links-about) can affect engagement measurements. A delivered status means the recipient's server accepted the message; it does not prove inbox placement.

## Dashboard controls

Open **Settings → Tracking** in your [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) and review the open and click tracking switches before sending. Configure the **Google Analytics** toggle in the same tab when using that integration.

Check [plans and billing](/account/plans-billing) for analytics access and retention: Free has a 30-day demo, Basic has 30 days of history, and Pro and Enterprise have 365 days. Custom tracking domains and webhooks have their own plan allowances.

## API controls

Use `consumerKey` and `consumerSecret` headers with a key that has **Statistics & Management APIs** enabled. Paths are relative to `https://pro.api.serversmtp.com/api/v2`.

| Method | Endpoint | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/tools` | List tools and their enabled state |
| `PUT` | `/tools/clickTracking/{action}` | Enable or disable click tracking |
| `PUT` | `/tools/openingTracking/{action}` | Enable or disable open tracking |

Replace `{action}` with `enable` or `disable`. Use the tools list to read current state. See the [analytics API reference](/api-reference/analytics) for request examples.

## Custom tracking domains

Use a domain you control for tracking URLs and open-tracking images. Free offers a 30-day demo, Basic allows two custom tracking domains, and Pro and Enterprise allow unlimited domains, as listed on [current pricing](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/).

Follow the [custom tracking domain guide](/deliverability/custom-tracking-domain) to configure DNS and domain rotation. At least one of open or click tracking must be enabled to use the domain.

## Event delivery

Use [webhooks](/analytics/webhooks) to receive available delivery and engagement events in your application. Review which events are enabled for your account before designing your integration around them.
