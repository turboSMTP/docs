turboSMTP automatically records every meaningful interaction your emails generate — from the moment a message leaves your account to when a recipient opens it or clicks a link. The advanced analytics dashboard gives you both aggregate rates and per-recipient event data, so you always know exactly how your email program is performing in real time.

## What you can track

turboSMTP captures the following events for every email you send:

- **Email delivery status** — whether the message was accepted, deferred, or bounced by the recipient's mail server
- **Opens** — date, time, and first-open data for each recipient who views your message
- **Clicks** — per-link and per-recipient click events showing exactly which URLs were clicked and when
- **Bounces** — hard and soft bounces with the error response from the receiving server
- **Spam complaints** — recipients who mark your message as spam via their email client's report button
- **Unsubscribes** — recipients who opt out via your unsubscribe link

## How tracking works

turboSMTP's tracking system is fully automatic — no additional setup is required on your part.

**Open tracking** works by injecting a small, transparent 1×1 pixel image into the HTML body of your email. When a recipient's email client loads the image, turboSMTP records an open event along with the timestamp, recipient address, and user agent.

**Click tracking** works by rewriting each link in your email to pass through turboSMTP's tracking server. When a recipient clicks a link, turboSMTP logs the event — capturing the URL, timestamp, IP address, and user agent — and then immediately redirects the recipient to the original destination. The redirect is seamless and adds no perceptible delay.

## Enable or disable tracking

Click and open tracking are both **enabled by default** on all turboSMTP accounts. You can toggle them at any time without changing your sending configuration.

**Via the dashboard:** Go to your [turboSMTP dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) → **Settings** and toggle the click tracking and open tracking switches.

**Via the API:** Tracking preferences can be toggled programmatically. Use the `PUT` method against the click-tracking and open-tracking control endpoints under your account settings path. Include your API key in the `Authorization` header (raw key — no `Bearer` prefix). See the [API reference](/api-reference/analytics) for full endpoint details.

<Note>
  Tracking requires HTML emails. Plain-text-only emails cannot be tracked for opens or clicks, because neither a tracking pixel nor rewritten links can be embedded in plain text.
</Note>

## Custom tracking domain

By default, turboSMTP rewrites your tracking links through a shared tracking domain. To keep your brand consistent and improve deliverability, you can configure a **custom tracking domain** so that tracking URLs appear under your own domain rather than turboSMTP's.

See the [Custom Tracking Domain guide](/deliverability/custom-tracking-domain) for step-by-step setup instructions.
