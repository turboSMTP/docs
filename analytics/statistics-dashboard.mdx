The turboSMTP analytics dashboard gives you a complete, real-time picture of your email program's performance. From high-level summary metrics down to the status of every individual message, you have the data you need to monitor deliverability, troubleshoot issues, and prove the impact of your email campaigns — all without writing a single line of code.

## Overview metrics

The main dashboard panel displays aggregate statistics across your account for any date range you select:

- **Emails sent** — total volume dispatched through your account
- **Delivered** — messages accepted by the recipient's mail server
- **Opened** — unique recipients who opened your message (requires HTML email with tracking enabled)
- **Clicked** — recipients who clicked at least one link in your message
- **Bounced** — messages that could not be delivered (hard and soft bounces combined)
- **Spam complaints** — recipients who reported your message as spam via their email client

These headline numbers update in real time as turboSMTP processes and tracks your sends.

## Email feed

Below the overview metrics, the **email feed** lists every individual email your account has sent. For each message you can see:

- Recipient email address
- Subject line
- Current status (see [Status definitions](#status-definitions) below)
- Timestamp of the last status change
- Full event history for that message (click the row to expand)

The feed lets you drill into a single recipient's journey — from the moment the message was queued through delivery, open, and any clicks — making it easy to diagnose delivery issues or confirm a specific email was received.

## Status definitions

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **NEW** | The message has been queued and is waiting to be sent. |
| **DEFER** | Delivery has been temporarily deferred. turboSMTP is retrying automatically. |
| **SUCCESS** | The message was accepted by the recipient's mail server (delivered). |
| **OPEN** | The recipient opened the email (tracking pixel loaded). |
| **CLICK** | The recipient clicked a tracked link in the email. |
| **REPORT** | The recipient marked the email as spam. |
| **FAIL** | Hard bounce — the message could not be delivered and will not be retried (e.g., address does not exist). |
| **SYSFAIL** | System-level bounce — a technical issue prevented delivery; not caused by the recipient's address. |
| **UNSUB** | The recipient clicked an unsubscribe link and has been added to the suppression list. |

## Date range and filters

Use the **date range picker** at the top of the Statistics page to focus on any time window — today, the last 7 days, a custom range, or any period since account creation.

Apply **status filters** to narrow the feed to only the events you care about. For example, filter by `FAIL` to review all hard bounces, or by `REPORT` to audit spam complaints. You can combine date range and status filters simultaneously.

## Export data

To work with your statistics outside the dashboard, export your email data as a CSV file.

**From the dashboard:** Click the **Export CSV** button on the Statistics page to download all filtered results.

**Via the API:** Retrieve email analytics programmatically with the following endpoint:

```bash Terminal
GET https://pro.api.serversmtp.com/api/v2/analytics/emails/csv
```

Include your API key in the `Authorization` header (raw key — do **not** prefix with `Bearer`) and pass query parameters to filter by date range, status, or recipient.

<Tip>
  Use the statistics API to pull metrics programmatically and integrate turboSMTP data into your own dashboards, reporting tools, or data warehouse. See the [Analytics API reference](/api-reference/analytics) for full parameter documentation.
</Tip>

## Activity reports

turboSMTP can automatically send you **scheduled activity reports** summarizing your account's email activity. Configure the report frequency — **daily**, **weekly**, or **monthly** — and turboSMTP will email the summary to your account address on schedule.

Activity reports include the same metrics shown in the dashboard overview (sent, delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, spam complaints) condensed into a convenient digest, so you stay informed even when you're not actively logged in to the dashboard. Configure scheduled reports from **Settings → Activity Reports** in your dashboard.
