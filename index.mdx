## Global and EU hosts

turboSMTP is an Italian company with European sending infrastructure. Use `pro.turbo-smtp.com` or `pro.eu.turbo-smtp.com` for SMTP, and `api.turbo-smtp.com` or `api.eu.turbo-smtp.com` for HTTP sending. See [hosts and data residency](/api-reference/base-urls) to choose your route.
