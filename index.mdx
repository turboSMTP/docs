## Get up and running in three steps

Everything you need to start sending email with turboSMTP — from signup to your first delivered message — takes only a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for free">
    Create your turboSMTP account at [serversmtp.com/en/signup](https://serversmtp.com/en/signup). No credit card is required. Your free plan includes 6,000 emails per month, ready to use immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your API keys from the dashboard">
    Log in to your [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) and navigate to **Settings → Integrations → API Keys**. Copy your **Consumer Key** and **Consumer Secret** — you'll need these to authenticate both SMTP relay connections and REST API requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first email via SMTP or API">
    Point your application at `pro.turbo-smtp.com` (port 587) using your Consumer Key as the username and your Consumer Secret as the password, or call the REST API endpoint at `https://api.turbo-smtp.com/api/v2/mail/send`. Either path gets your first email delivered in seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
