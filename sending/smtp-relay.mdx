Use the turboSMTP SMTP relay with an email client, CMS, or application that supports authenticated SMTP. The relay accepts and queues your outbound messages, then handles delivery attempts. Your plan determines sending allowances and access to features such as dedicated IPs and analytics retention.

## Connection settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Global host | `pro.turbo-smtp.com` |
| EU host | `pro.eu.turbo-smtp.com` |
| STARTTLS ports | `587` (recommended), `2525`, `25` |
| Implicit TLS ports | `465`, `25025` |
| Username | Your **Consumer Key** |
| Password | Your **Consumer Secret** |
| Key permission | **SMTP Sending** |

Both hosts support the same ports. turboSMTP is an Italian company with European sending infrastructure; use `pro.eu.turbo-smtp.com` for the EU route. See [data residency](/api-reference/base-urls#gdpr-and-data-residency) for contractual considerations.

## Choose encryption

- On `587`, `2525`, or `25`, connect with SMTP and require a STARTTLS upgrade before authentication.
- On `465` or `25025`, start the connection with TLS enabled (implicit TLS).
- Use `2525` when your network blocks `587`. Hosting providers often block `25`.

For libraries such as Nodemailer, `secure: true` means implicit TLS and belongs with ports `465` or `25025`. On port `587`, use `secure: false` together with `requireTLS: true` to require STARTTLS.

## Configure and verify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create credentials">
    In the [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com), open **Settings → Integrations → API Keys → Edit**. Create a key with **SMTP Sending** enabled. Copy the Consumer Secret when it is shown and store it securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your client's outgoing mail server">
    Enter a host, port, and matching encryption mode from the table. Enter the Consumer Key as the username and Consumer Secret as the password. Your dashboard login password is not the SMTP password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a message">
    Send to an address you control. Open **Analytics** in the dashboard to inspect the delivery record. Successful SMTP authentication confirms your credentials; a queued message is not yet proof of delivery to the recipient's server or inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [SMTP setup](/get-started/smtp-configuration) for client instructions and [common errors](/troubleshooting/common-errors) for connection and authentication troubleshooting.

## Plans and limits

The Free plan allows up to 200 emails per day. Paid sending allowances depend on your chosen plan. Dedicated IPs are a Pro feature rather than part of every relay account. Check the [pricing comparison](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/) for current plan limits, trials, and support channels.

Both SMTP and the [HTTP send API](/sending/email-api) support email bodies and attachments. Choose the protocol that fits your application.
