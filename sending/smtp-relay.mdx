An SMTP relay is an outgoing mail server that accepts your messages and routes them to their destination — handling authentication, queuing, retries, and delivery tracking on your behalf. Instead of relying on your ISP's limited SMTP server (which often blocks bulk sending, lacks analytics, and can hurt your sender reputation), you point your email client or application at turboSMTP's professional relay infrastructure. turboSMTP gives you dedicated sending IPs, real-time statistics, bounce management, and 99.9% uptime — all without managing your own mail server.

## Connection settings

Use these settings in any email client, application, or plugin that asks for SMTP server details.

| Setting           | Value                        |
|-------------------|------------------------------|
| **SMTP host**     | `pro.turbo-smtp.com`         |
| **Port (TLS)**    | `587` (recommended)          |
| **Port (SSL)**    | `465`                        |
| **Port (fallback)**| `2525`                      |
| **Authentication**| Required                     |
| **Username** | Your Consumer Key |
| **Password**      | Your Consumer Secret         |
| **Encryption**    | STARTTLS or SSL/TLS          |

<Note>
  Your SMTP username is your **Consumer Key** and your SMTP password is your **Consumer Secret**, not your account email and login password. Find both in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) under **Settings → Integrations → API Keys**.
</Note>

## Supported ports

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Port 587 — STARTTLS (Recommended)">
    Port 587 is the standard submission port for authenticated SMTP. It starts an unencrypted connection and upgrades to TLS via the STARTTLS command. This port works on virtually every network and is the recommended choice for all new integrations.

    ```text Connection details
    Host: pro.turbo-smtp.com
    Port: 587
    Encryption: STARTTLS / TLS
    Auth: Required
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Port 465 — SSL/TLS">
    Port 465 wraps the entire connection in SSL/TLS from the start (sometimes called "implicit TLS"). Use this port if your client requires SSL mode or explicitly supports SMTPS. It is a solid alternative when STARTTLS is not supported.

    ```text Connection details
    Host: pro.turbo-smtp.com
    Port: 465
    Encryption: SSL/TLS (implicit)
    Auth: Required
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Port 2525 — Fallback">
    Port 2525 is a non-standard fallback port that mirrors port 587 behavior. Use it only when ports 587 and 465 are both blocked by your ISP, corporate firewall, or cloud provider (AWS EC2 and similar environments commonly block these ports).

    ```text Connection details
    Host: pro.turbo-smtp.com
    Port: 2525
    Encryption: STARTTLS / TLS
    Auth: Required
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to configure

Follow these steps to start sending through turboSMTP's SMTP relay.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your turboSMTP account">
    Sign up for free at [serversmtp.com/en/signup](https://serversmtp.com/en/signup). The free plan includes 6,000 emails per month — no credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your Consumer Secret">
    Log in to the [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) and navigate to **Settings → Integrations → API Keys**. Copy your **Consumer Secret** — this is the password you will use for SMTP authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your client or application">
    Open your email client, plugin, or application's SMTP settings. Enter the connection details from the table above, using your Consumer Key as the username and your Consumer Secret as the password. For step-by-step walkthroughs for specific clients and platforms, see the [SMTP Configuration guide](/get-started/smtp-configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test email">
    Send a test message and verify it appears in your [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com) statistics. If it does not arrive, check that your credentials are correct and that your chosen port is not blocked by your firewall.
  </Step>
</Steps>
