## How it works

When you send an email through your own mail server, your messages compete with billions of other senders for inbox placement. ISPs and spam filters judge your domain and IP reputation on every send. An SMTP relay service like turboSMTP solves this by routing your outgoing email through its own trusted, authenticated infrastructure, so your messages benefit from established ISP relationships and whitelisted IP addresses.

turboSMTP gives you two integration paths:

- **SMTP relay** — Update the outgoing mail server settings in your application, mail client, or CMS to point at `pro.turbo-smtp.com` on port 587 (or 465 for SSL, 2525 as a fallback). Authenticate with your Consumer Key as the username and your Consumer Secret as the password. No code changes are needed beyond configuration.
- **REST API** — Send email programmatically by making HTTPS POST requests to `https://api.turbo-smtp.com/api/v2/mail/send`. For EU data residency, use `https://api.eu.turbo-smtp.com/api/v2/mail/send` instead. Authenticate each request with your `consumerKey` and `consumerSecret` headers. This path is ideal for custom applications, microservices, and high-volume automated sending.

Both paths route through the same delivery infrastructure and give you access to the same analytics, tracking, and management features.

## Key features

turboSMTP includes a complete set of tools to help you send, monitor, and optimize email delivery:

- **Real-time delivery tracking** — See the status of every sent message — delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, or unsubscribed — in your live analytics dashboard.
- **Bounce, spam, and unsubscribe management** — Track soft and hard bounces, spam complaints, and unsubscribes address-by-address with a real-time feed. Automatically suppress unsubscribed contacts from future sends.
- **SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication** — Publish DNS records that prove your emails are legitimate, protect your domain from spoofing, and improve inbox placement rates.
- **Smart Routing** — Automatically distribute email across multiple IP addresses and routes to overcome deliverability issues, manage high volume, and isolate different email streams.
- **Custom Tracking Domains** — Replace the default shared tracking domain with your own subdomain to protect your sender reputation from other users and build trust with your recipients.
- **Event webhooks** — Receive real-time HTTP notifications to your server for every email event, enabling you to trigger workflows, update CRMs, and react to delivery outcomes instantly.
- **Sub-accounts & white label** — Create sub-accounts to split sending quotas across team members or resell turboSMTP capacity to your clients under your own brand.
- **Email Validation Tool** — Validate your contact list in bulk via the dashboard, in real time via the WordPress plugin, or programmatically via the Email Validation API before you launch a campaign.
- **European servers and GDPR compliance**: Keep email data inside the EU with dedicated European servers and IP addresses, operated by an Italian company.
- **24/7 multi-language support** — Reach a real person any time by ticket, live chat, or phone — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
