## Create an account and credentials

Register through the [dashboard](https://dashboard.serversmtp.com). Free-plan activation is subject to account review; check [free-plan setup](/get-started/free-trial) for the steps. The Free allowance is up to 200 emails per day. See [pricing](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/) for the current validity period and feature limits.

Create credentials under **Settings → Integrations → API Keys → Edit**. Use the **Consumer Key as your SMTP username** and **Consumer Secret as your SMTP password**. For HTTP calls, pass them as `consumerKey` and `consumerSecret` headers. Enable the sending or management permission needed by your integration.

## Global and EU sending

turboSMTP is an Italian company with European sending infrastructure. Choose the appropriate host:

| Protocol | Global | EU |
|---|---|---|
| SMTP | `pro.turbo-smtp.com` | `pro.eu.turbo-smtp.com` |
| Send API | `https://api.turbo-smtp.com/api/v2/mail/send` | `https://api.eu.turbo-smtp.com/api/v2/mail/send` |

Both SMTP hosts support STARTTLS on `587`, `2525`, and `25`, and implicit TLS on `465` and `25025`. Use the [base URL guide](/api-reference/base-urls) for management hosts and data-residency considerations.

## After your first send

- [Authenticate your domain](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc) with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
- Open **Analytics** to inspect delivery records, or receive [event webhooks](/analytics/webhooks).
- Manage bounces and complaints through [suppressions](/deliverability/bounce-spam-management).
- Use [email validation](/account/email-validation) to check contact addresses before sending.
- Configure [sub-accounts](/account/subaccounts) when you manage sending for multiple customers.

Feature availability, retention, dedicated IPs, and support channels depend on your plan. See [plans and billing](/account/plans-billing) before relying on a feature or trial.
