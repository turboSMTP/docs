For many senders a single outbound IP address is sufficient — until it isn't. As your sending volume grows, or if an inbox provider starts deferring mail from one IP, a single-IP setup quickly becomes a bottleneck or a liability. turboSMTP's Smart Routing feature solves this by giving your email multiple paths to the internet: it randomizes delivery attempts across a pool of sending IPs so that a deferral on one address does not stop your campaign in its tracks.

## How Smart Routing works

Smart Routing randomizes each individual delivery attempt across a shared pool of turboSMTP IP addresses. When a receiving mail server defers or temporarily rejects a message from one IP, the next delivery attempt for that message comes from a different IP in the pool. This removes the need to manually request an IP change whenever you encounter deliverability issues with a particular provider.

For more complex situations, Smart Routing also allows granular routing adjustments: you can shift traffic for specific recipient domains onto routing paths that are currently achieving better delivery rates. Because configuration changes take effect instantly, your team can respond quickly to deliverability problems and minimize the impact on your overall sending program.

## When to use Smart Routing

Smart Routing is most valuable when a single IP address can no longer meet your needs. The following scenarios are the most common triggers.

## Smart Routing vs Dedicated IP Pools

Smart Routing and Dedicated IP Pools both solve multi-IP routing problems, but they serve different sender profiles. Choose based on your volume, your need for reputation control, and how much operational overhead you can manage.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Smart Routing">
    Smart Routing uses turboSMTP's **shared IP pool**, so you benefit from the established reputation of IPs that other well-behaved senders also use.

    - No IP warmup required — the shared pool is already warmed
    - Automatic rotation with no manual configuration per send
    - Changes to routing rules take effect instantly
    - Ideal for low-to-mid volume senders and anyone who needs quick, low-effort deliverability improvements
    - Contact turboSMTP support to enable
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dedicated IP Pools">
    Dedicated IP Pools give you a range of IPs used **only by your account**, putting full reputation control in your hands.

    - Complete isolation from other senders — your reputation depends solely on your own sending behaviour
    - Requires IP warmup before sending at full volume
    - Requires careful list hygiene and consistent sending patterns to maintain IP health
    - Best suited to high-volume senders who can sustain the volume needed to keep dedicated IPs warm
    - Contact turboSMTP support to set up
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Enable Smart Routing

Smart Routing is not a self-service toggle — it requires turboSMTP's team to configure the routing rules for your account so that they are tailored to your sending patterns and recipient mix.

To request Smart Routing for your account, contact the turboSMTP support team:

- **Support portal:** [serversmtp.com/en/contact-us](https://serversmtp.com/en/contact-us)
- **Live chat:** available from the turboSMTP dashboard and website
- **Phone and ticket:** turboSMTP support is available 24/7

When you reach out, describe your sending volume, the inbox providers you are having trouble with (if any), and whether you need multiple routing streams for different email types. The support team will configure Smart Routing and confirm when it is active on your account.

<Note>
  Changes to Smart Routing configuration take effect instantly — there is no propagation delay. Once the support team applies a routing rule change, it applies to the very next delivery attempt.
</Note>
