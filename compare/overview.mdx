If you are evaluating email providers or planning to switch, these pages compare turboSMTP with other popular services and walk you through a migration step by step.

## A typical migration in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Sign up for free](https://serversmtp.com/en/signup) (6,000 emails per month, no credit card) and generate your [API keys](/get-started/api-keys).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate your domain">
    Add turboSMTP's SPF and DKIM records next to your current provider's records. See [SPF, DKIM, and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap credentials or endpoints">
    Point your SMTP host or API calls at turboSMTP. Choose the EU endpoints if your data must stay in Europe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ramp up and cut over">
    Move a share of traffic first, watch [statistics](/analytics/statistics-dashboard), then send everything through turboSMTP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Planning a large move? turboSMTP's team helps businesses migrate from other providers. [Contact support](https://serversmtp.com/contact-us/) to plan your switch.
</Tip>
