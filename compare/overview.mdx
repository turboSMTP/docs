## Plan your migration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your plan and credentials">
    Review [current pricing](https://serversmtp.com/pricing/) and create [API keys](/get-started/api-keys) with the sending permissions you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate your domain">
    Add turboSMTP to your existing single SPF record and publish its DKIM record. Keep your current provider authorized during cutover. See [SPF, DKIM, and DMARC](/deliverability/spf-dkim-dmarc).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move settings and suppression data">
    Update SMTP or API configuration, import suppressions, and adapt webhook handling. Verify regional and feature availability before moving traffic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ramp up and cut over">
    Move a share of traffic first and inspect [Analytics](/analytics/statistics-dashboard). Retire the old provider after its queue has drained.
  </Step>
</Steps>
